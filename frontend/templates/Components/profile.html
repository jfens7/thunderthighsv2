Volatility Score Guide (1–100)

Measures how closely a player performs relative to rating expectations.

1 – 25 • The Metronome Low Volatility

Rock-solid consistency. Beats lower-rated players reliably, loses to higher-rated players as expected, and rarely experiences random blowouts.

26 – 55 • Standard Form Normal Volatility

Typical competitive club form. Capable of pulling off a solid upset on a good night or dropping a tight 5-setter on an off night.

56 – 80 • Wildcard High Volatility

Streaky and dangerous. Form fluctuates heavily from match to match—ratings rarely predict which version of this player is showing up at the table.

81 – 100 • Pure Chaos Extreme Volatility

Could push Fan Zhendong to 7 sets at 7:30 PM and lose 3-0 to the lowest-rated player in the club at 8:15 PM. Completely immune to expectations.
